Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

TCG Creator Dashboard is a private analytics dashboard operated by 7 Cards in Hand TCG. This policy explains how connected social media data is used.

Information Accessed

When authorized by the account owner, the dashboard may access basic profile information and public content analytics from connected platforms, such as video titles, post URLs, view counts, like counts, comment counts, share counts, follower or subscriber counts, and related performance metrics.

How Information Is Used

The information is used only to display content performance and analytics inside the private dashboard. It helps the owner/operator understand which social media content is performing well.

Storage

Access tokens and synced analytics data are stored locally or on the private server running the dashboard. They are not intentionally shared with third parties.

Sharing

The dashboard does not sell, rent, or share connected account data with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

Revoking Access

You can revoke access at any time through the connected platform's account security or app permissions settings. After access is revoked, the dashboard will no longer be able to sync new data from that platform.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the owner/operator of 7 Cards in Hand TCG.
